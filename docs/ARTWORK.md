# Original artwork

Created with the built-in image-generation tool using the imagegen skill. Assets were copied into this project; there are no dependencies on the original generated-images folder.

- `public/art/hero.png`: Original cinematic anime background: a young adult woman in a cream jacket and red scarf on a rooftop above a Japanese coastal city at blue hour. Teal mountains, warm windows, orange sunset; dark negative space at left. Detailed painterly texture, no text or logos.
- `public/art/forest.png`: Original anime poster: a swordswoman with a red ribbon beneath a vermilion torii gate among ancient cedars at twilight. Blue fireflies and fog, navy/red/ivory palette, no text or logos.
- `public/art/orbit.png`: Original animated adventure poster: a small orange fox astronaut in a cream spacesuit among green planets and luminous jellyfish in midnight-blue space. Teal and amber, painterly texture, no text or logos.

These images are fictional catalog artwork. They are not claims that playable films exist.
